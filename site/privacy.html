Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Applies to SimpleSwing for iPhone, iPad, Android and Wear OS

In short: SimpleSwing doesn't collect any of your data.

  • There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your swing videos, notes and settings stay on your own devices.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

SimpleSwing is made by [Developer name], an individual developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with the camera, microphone and other features on your device, and where your recordings go. If anything here is unclear, contact me at [contact email].

What the app uses, and why

FeatureWhy it's usedWhere the data goes
Camera To film your swing. Stays on your device. See "Your swings" below.
Microphone To hear the sound of the club hitting the ball, so the app can save your swing automatically. Saved clips also include their soundtrack. The sound is analysed live on your device and is not stored on its own. The soundtrack inside a saved clip stays on your device with the clip.
Motion sensor (accelerometer) To show whether the phone is level. Used live on your device. Not stored.
Photos / gallery (add only) To save a swing to your Photos or gallery, only when you tap Save to Photos. Your device's photo library. SimpleSwing asks only to add videos and doesn't read your existing photos or videos.
Local network Optional. To link two of your own phones, so a second phone can film another angle. Directly between your two phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. See "Second phone" below.
Watch (Wear OS) Optional. To use your watch as a remote, and to sense your wrist swing and tempo. Directly between your watch and your phone. See "Watch" below.

You can turn off any of these permissions in your device's settings at any time. Without the camera the app can't record. Without the microphone it can't save swings automatically, but you can still save them with the SAVE button, a volume button or a remote.

Your swings

  • The live camera isn't stored. While the Capture screen is open, the app keeps only the last few seconds of video in a short rolling buffer that is constantly overwritten. Nothing is kept unless a swing is saved.
  • Saved swings stay in the app's own storage on your device. That includes the video, a thumbnail, and details such as frame rate, club, result tags, favourites, drawings and tempo.
  • Photos and sharing happen only when you choose. A swing goes to your Photos or gallery only when you tap Save to Photos. When you tap Share, your device's share sheet opens and you choose where the video goes. After that, the app or service you pick handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Deleting: delete swings in the app, or set Settings → Storage to delete old swings automatically. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Copies you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are not affected.

Backups

SimpleSwing has no backup or cloud service of its own, and I never receive your backups. Your device's own backup may include some of the app's data, under your account and the backup provider's terms:

  • iPhone and iPad: if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your saved swings, notes and settings may be included in that backup.
  • Android: your swings (videos and their notes and tags) are excluded from Android's backup and from phone-to-phone transfer. Your app settings may be included in your device's own backup under your Google account.
  • Wear OS watch: the watch app stores nothing that needs backing up, and its backup is turned off.

Second phone (optional)

You can use a second phone running SimpleSwing as a second camera. This works only between your own phones on the same local network: the same Wi-Fi, or one phone's hotspot. On iPhone and iPad, the phones can also connect over Apple's direct peer-to-peer Wi-Fi.

  • Pairing code: the camera phone shows a 6-digit pairing code. You type it on your main phone the first time you connect. The camera phone only accepts a main phone that knows its code, so other people on the same network can't control it.
  • Discovery: while a phone is set as the camera, it announces itself on the local network so your main phone can find it. The announcement includes the phone's name (for example, the name you gave it in Android settings, or "iPhone"), so other devices on that network can see that name.
  • What's sent: commands to save a swing, timing messages that keep the two phones in step, the app's recording state, and the clip of each saved swing, which is copied to your main phone.
  • What isn't: nothing goes over the internet, to a server, to the developer or to anyone else. The connection is direct between your phones and isn't encrypted, so, as with any local-network traffic, use a network you trust or one phone's hotspot.

On iPhone and iPad you'll be asked for Local Network permission the first time. On Android the app uses the Internet permission only to open this local connection.

Watch (optional, Wear OS)

  • As a remote: tapping the watch tells your phone to save the swing.
  • Sensing your swing: while your phone is on the Capture screen, the watch reads its motion sensor (gyroscope) to recognise a swing and time your backswing and downswing. This is worked out on the watch. Raw sensor readings never leave it.
  • What's sent: the watch and phone exchange only short messages: "save" taps, the time and peak speed of each wrist swing, tempo timings, status and timing checks. They go through the Wearable Data Layer, Google's link between your paired phone and watch. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • SimpleSwing doesn't read or write Health Connect, Google Fit or any other health records, and it doesn't use your heart rate or location.

Crash reports

SimpleSwing contains no crash-reporting or analytics code. If you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings (on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements; on Android, Google's usage and diagnostics setting), Apple or Google may give me anonymous crash reports and performance statistics. This is controlled by Apple's and Google's own settings and privacy policies, and you can turn it off there.

What I don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There's none to share.
  • No use of your location, contacts or existing photos.

Children

SimpleSwing isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because I don't collect or hold any of your data, there's nothing for me to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Your data is under your control on your own devices: delete swings in the app, or uninstall it. If you have a question about privacy, contact me and I'll answer it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, I'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships, and describe it in the app's release notes. If a change ever meant collecting data, the app would ask you first.
