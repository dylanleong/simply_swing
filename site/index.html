Hit the ball. Watch your swing.

SimpleSwing is a hands-free golf swing recorder. Put your phone on a tripod and hit balls. It hears the strike, keeps the seconds before it and replays your swing in slow motion, while you stay in your stance.

Get help Privacy policy

What it does

Saves itself

Impact sound plus movement in your golfer box. Neighbours' shots are ignored.

Slow motion

120 fps, or 240 fps on phones that support it, with frame-by-frame review.

Draw and compare

Plane lines, circles and angles, grid and mirror, and two swings side by side.

Tag every shot

Club and result in one tap, then filter to find what your slices have in common.

Two angles

Optional second phone films down the line and face on together.

Remotes and watch

Volume buttons, Bluetooth camera remotes, and a Wear OS watch with wrist tempo.

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • Your swings stay on your phone unless you save or share them.
  • Works fully offline.
